ℹ️ About the Apriori Algorithm

The Apriori algorithm is a classic algorithm used for mining frequent itemsets and deriving association rules from transactional databases. It operates on the principle that any subset of a frequent itemset must also be frequent, which dramatically reduces the search space.

Key Metrics Explained

Support

Support shows how often items appear together. For the rule A→B, it means the share of transactions that contain both A and B.

A high support value indicates the rule is highly relevant to the overall dataset.

Confidence

Confidence measures how reliable a rule is. It is the probability that a transaction contains 𝐵 given that it already contains 𝐴.

For example, a confidence of 0.8 means that 80% of customers who buy A also proceed to buy B.

Lift

Lift measures how much more likely 𝐵 is to be purchased when 𝐴 is purchased, compared to purchasing 𝐵 independently. It shows the strength of the rule above random chance.
